Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit abitrolabs.com, book a call or contact us, why we collect it, who we share it with and what rights you have. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Danish Data Protection Act (databeskyttelsesloven).

1. Data controller

Abitrolabs, founded by August Havsteen-Mikkelsen, is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us about anything in this policy by sending a direct message to @abitrolabs on Instagram.

2. What we collect and where it comes from

  • Booking data – when you book a call through our Calendly calendar: your name, email address, the time you choose, and anything you write in the booking form (for example your company, website or revenue). You give us this directly.
  • Messages – when you message us on Instagram: your username, profile information that Instagram shows us and the content of your messages.
  • Call notes – notes we take during a call about your business, your store and your marketing. If you give us access to analytics, store or ad accounts as part of an audit, we only use that access for the audit.
  • Technical data – when you visit the website, our hosting provider automatically logs your IP address, browser type, device, the pages requested and the time of the request.

We do not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies on this website. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

3. Why we use it and our legal basis

  • To schedule and hold the call you booked, prepare for it and follow up afterwards. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in following up on business enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To answer your messages. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To carry out an audit or an engagement you have agreed to. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the website secure and working. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To meet legal obligations, for example bookkeeping and tax rules. Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Where we rely on legitimate interest, you can object at any time (see section 8).

4. Who we share it with

We only share personal data with service providers that process it on our behalf under a data processing agreement, or where the law requires it:

  • Vercel Inc. (USA) – website hosting and server logs
  • Calendly LLC (USA) – booking calendar, confirmations and reminders
  • Framer B.V. – delivers some of the images shown on this website, which means your browser sends your IP address to Framer's servers

Instagram is run by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. When you message us there, Meta processes your data as an independent controller under Instagram's own privacy policy.

We may also disclose data to public authorities, advisers (for example our accountant or lawyer) or a buyer of the business, where this is required by law or necessary to protect our legal rights.

5. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Vercel and Calendly are based in the United States. Transfers to them are made under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, which require them to protect your data to an EU standard.

6. Cookies

This website does not set any cookies of its own. The fonts are hosted on our own server, so no data is sent to Google when you load a page.

The booking calendar is provided by Calendly. When it loads, Calendly shows its own cookie banner and only sets non-essential cookies if you accept them. See Calendly's cookie notice for details.

7. How long we keep it

  • Booking data, messages and call notes if we do not end up working together: deleted no later than 12 months after our last contact.
  • Client data if we work together: kept for the duration of the engagement and up to 3 years afterwards, so that we can document our work and handle any claims.
  • Accounting records (invoices and related data): kept for 5 years from the end of the financial year, as required by the Danish Bookkeeping Act (bogføringsloven).
  • Server logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period, normally no more than 30 days.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • get access to the personal data we hold about you
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data deleted
  • have the processing of your data restricted
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest
  • receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (data portability)
  • withdraw any consent you have given, without affecting processing before the withdrawal

To use any of these rights, message us on Instagram. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we will reply within one month. Some rights have exceptions, for example where we must keep data to meet a legal obligation.

You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet, Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, Denmark) or to the data protection authority where you live or work. We would appreciate the chance to resolve any concern with you first.

9. Security

The website is served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). We limit access to personal data to the people who need it, and we use providers that maintain appropriate technical and organisational security measures.

10. Children

Our services are for businesses. This website is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, providers or the law change. The current version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top.
