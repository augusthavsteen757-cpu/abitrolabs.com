Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Abitrolabs, founded by August Havsteen-Mikkelsen. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us by sending a direct message to @abitrolabs on Instagram.

What we collect

  • When you book a call: your name, email address, the time you choose and anything you write in the booking form. Bookings are handled by our scheduling provider, Calendly.
  • When you message us on Instagram: your username and whatever you send us. Instagram is run by Meta, under Meta's own privacy policy.
  • When you visit the website: our hosting provider automatically logs basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers on this website, and we do not sell your data.

Why we use it

  • To schedule and hold the call you booked, and to follow up afterwards. This is a step you ask us to take before a possible agreement (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep the website secure and working (our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To meet our legal obligations, for example accounting rules if we end up working together (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Vercel – website hosting
  • Calendly – call booking and calendar invites
  • Google Fonts – loads the fonts on this page, which means your browser sends your IP address to Google
  • Framer – serves some of the images on this page

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where data leaves the EU/EEA, the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies. The embedded Calendly booking calendar may set cookies that are needed for the booking to work.

How long we keep it

If we don't end up working together, we delete booking details and messages within 12 months of our last contact. If we do work together, we keep what we need for the engagement and for as long as accounting law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. To do this, message us on Instagram and we will reply within one month.

If you think we have handled your data incorrectly, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet, or the data protection authority in your own country.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
